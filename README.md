# My Favorite Movie of All Time

## Flipped (2010)

**Flipped** is my favorite movie of all time. It is a coming-of-age romantic comedy-drama about two young neighbors, Juli Baker and Bryce Loski, who have completely different perspectives on each other. What I love most about this movie is how it shows that feelings, opinions, and first impressions can change as we grow older and understand people better.

### Why I Love This Movie
- The story is simple but meaningful and impactful.
- I love how Juli is confident, independent, and not afraid to stand by what she believes in.
- It teaches us not to judge people based only on their appearance or first impressions.
- The movie has a nostalgic and comforting feeling that makes it special to me.

### My Favorite Characters
1. Juli Baker
2. Bryce Loski
3. Chet Duncan

> "Some of us get dipped in flat, some in satin, some in gloss. But every once in a while, you find someone who's iridescent."

### My Favorite Thing About the Movie

My favorite thing about *Flipped* is how the story is told from both Juli's and Bryce's perspectives. It makes you realize that two people can experience the same situations differently. I also love how Juli sees the beauty in things that other people often overlook, especially the sycamore tree.
